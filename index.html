Parasitic Dino Survival

A fogbound prehistoric basin with near-black nights and no guns. Nineteen dinosaurs, one of each, every one taken by a parasite called the Graft. The Gravemaw stalks from cover, freezes when you look at it, and goes through trees to reach you.
